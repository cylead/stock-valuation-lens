HOW TO READ IT

Reference, not a price target

The orange line multiplies the selected per-share metric by P/E = 15 × [(1 + CAGR) ÷ 1.10]years × 0.6, capped at 60×. CAGR and years use the first and last positive annual observations within the active window; the 0.6 weight gives long historical growth runs partial credit.

  • Black below orange: price is below the formula reference.
  • Negative values create gaps instead of misleading ratios.
